\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Multiplicity-weighted Stochastic Attention generated longitudinal profiles from $23$ patients
with low marginal error, retained cross-visit covariance, and preserved the represented
subgroup patterns. The profiles were also consistent with a coagulation ODE model
calibrated on the same cohort. A model calibrated on synthetic profiles predicted held-out
real TGA measurements as well as one calibrated on real profiles. Direct sampling from
the same target distribution reproduced the Langevin sampler's fidelity and
membership-inference results. Together, these findings provide proof of concept for
using synthetic profiles in the modeling tasks tested here with very small longitudinal
cohorts.
