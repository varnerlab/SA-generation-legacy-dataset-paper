# Repository Instructions

Before editing the manuscript, supplement, or response to reviewers, read and follow
[`HOUSE_STYLE.md`](HOUSE_STYLE.md).

The house style is authoritative for new and reviewer-added prose. In particular, keep the
`paper/` and `arxiv/` manuscript copies synchronized, update the Markdown response to
reviewers when relevant, preserve reviewer color macros, and compile and verify the LaTeX
after editing.
