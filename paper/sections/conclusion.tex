\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have demonstrated that Stochastic Attention can generate synthetic longitudinal
patient data from very small clinical cohorts ($K{=}23$) that pass four levels of
validation: marginal plausibility, cross-visit covariance preservation, conditional
subgroup amplification, and mechanistic consistency with an independent ODE model of
the coagulation cascade. The SA framework preserves the geometry of the clinical data
manifold in regimes where parametric approaches such as multivariate normal sampling
fail due to rank deficiency. Combined with a rigorous multi-level validation framework,
this approach provides a practical path for generating clinically validated synthetic
cohorts to support computational research in rare conditions and small longitudinal
studies.
